Made by Tools > Match3 > Difficulty Report. Every level was played 400 times by each bot (base seed 1).

- **Random win**: win rate of a bot that plays any valid move. A lower bound.
- **Greedy win**: win rate of a bot that plays the move that advances the goals most. A rough skilled player.
- **Avg moves left**: average moves left on a win, greedy bot.
- **Rating** (greedy win rate): Easy above 80%, Medium 50-80%, Hard 20-50%, Very Hard below 20%.
- **Stars** are written as 2-star/3-star moves left. Suggested = 40th and 75th percentile of the greedy wins.
